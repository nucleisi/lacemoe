hi! this is a little page about my gender, attraction, and relationship stuff. i don't love labels that much since i don't like feeling confined in a 'box' (as well as the fact that i'm still figuring most this out), so take everything here as a "best current description" rather than a definitive definition of myself ^w^

gender

gender identity
girlnonbinaryguy

masculine-leaning, but not fully a guy i think. i don't have a strong sense of gender and most days i'd consider myself more agender than anything else, but sometimes i do feel more like a guy. perhaps some type of genderfluid? i don't know. definitely not a girl though... and even when i don't really feel like anything, i still prefer masculine pronouns and terms used on me, so defaulting to them either way is fine

presentation
feminineandrogynousmasculine

pretty much right in the middle. androgynous is the goal, even if circumstances don't always allow it. i'm okay with wearing feminine clothing (skirts, bows, long socks... not really anything else though, i don't know) but i would prefer to appear androgynous in general if possible...

attraction

i'm not super into labeling this one, but the closest terms i've found are achillean (men or masculine-aligned individuals attracted to primarily to men and masculine-aligned individuals, though the "primarily" is doing a lot of work for me here,,,) and probably demiromantic & demisexual (for me specifically, i don't really experience physical attraction unless there's already an established emotional connection. so like i don't really find anyone attractive until i actually know and care about them). these labels feel close-ish but not perfect, which is pretty much how all labels feel for me
